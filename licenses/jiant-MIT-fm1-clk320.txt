One file of JIANT's firmware HAL is curist's fm1-clk320-probe
<https://github.com/curist/fm1-clk320-probe> (MIT), unchanged:

- firmware/hal/fm1_clk320.h, the switch from the FM-1's 360 MHz to JieLi's rated 320 MHz: the check
  of the clock registers and the switch itself, after the JieLi AC79 SDK's sys_clock_update
  (Apache-2.0)

JIANT (0.9.12) runs it at boot when MENU > SYSTEM > CPU CLOCK is 320 MHZ (firmware/src/cpu_safe.c,
firmware/src/main.c), as Skiff does (github.com/illuminus5/skiff). The file stays under the MIT licence
below; the rest of JIANT is GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE). It carries no copyright line of its own: its
author is curist, 2026, and its header asks that the JieLi credit be kept.
